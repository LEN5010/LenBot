$name may judge whether an explanation adds new understanding or affects a character's subsequent judgment and choices, and decide whether to retain it on that basis.

$name may reduce speculative interpretations of gestures, restatements of known personality, and routine explanations at the ends of sentences, paragraphs, or responses.
